# Multi-Port Manifold (CAD Methodology 18)

A 3D mechanical model of a multi-port fluid manifold featuring a central domed chamber and patterned, angled side ports. 

**Design Tools:** SolidWorks 2024 (Revolve, Extrude, Circular Pattern, Cut-Revolve, Fillets).

**Files:** `.SLDPRT` (Native CAD), `.STEP` (Universal 3D), and `.JPG` (Render Preview).
