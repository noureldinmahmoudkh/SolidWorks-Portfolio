# Rectangular Containment Tray (CAD Methodology 29)

A 3D mechanical model of a shallow rectangular tray featuring angled walls and fully radiused interior and exterior edges for smooth surface transitions. 

**Design Tools:** SolidWorks 2024 (Boss-Extrude, Shell, Fillets).

**Files:** `.SLDPRT` (Native CAD), `.STEP` (Universal 3D), and `.JPG` (Render Preview).
