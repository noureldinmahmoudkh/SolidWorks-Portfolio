# Multi-Axis Fluid Manifold (CAD Methodology 32)

A 3D mechanical model of a complex pipe fitting designed for multi-directional fluid routing. The design features a central chamber, straight and swept flanged ports, and integrated mounting tabs.

**Design Tools:** SolidWorks 2024 (Boss-Extrude, Swept Boss/Base, Cut-Extrude, Swept Cut, Fillets).

**Files:** `.SLDPRT` (Native CAD), `.STEP` (Universal 3D), and `.JPG` (Render Preview).
