# Slotted Mounting Cover (CAD Methodology 24)

A 3D mechanical model of a circular mounting plate featuring curved adjustment slots, perimeter mounting tabs, and a hollowed interior structure. 

**Design Tools:** SolidWorks 2024 (Extrude, Shell, Complex Sketching).

**Files:** `.SLDPRT` (Native CAD), `.STEP` (Universal 3D), and `.JPG` (Render Preview).
